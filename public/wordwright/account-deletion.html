Delete your WordWright account

This page applies to WordWright: Idle Word Factory by Erkam Demirci.

Delete your account in the app (immediate)

  1. Open WordWright.
  2. Go to MORE → Settings → Account (also reachable from the "Your Shop's Deed" account screen).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion is permanent and immediate. It removes your account, your cloud save, and your Today's Front Page history. It cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account ID (and any linked Apple/Google association)
  • Your cloud save (shop state, Lexicon, Volumes)
  • Your Front Page scores and leaderboard entries

What is kept

  • Purchase records are retained by Apple / Google / RevenueCat as required for financial record-keeping; we keep no personal profile after deletion.
  • Aggregate, non-identifying analytics (e.g. total words printed by all players) cannot be traced back to you.

If you can't access the app

E-mail us at erkmdmrc@gmail.com from any address and include your leaderboard display name (if you set one); we will delete the account data manually.
